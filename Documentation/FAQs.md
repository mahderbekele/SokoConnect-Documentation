__SokoConnect Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)__
**For Customers**
1. What is SokoConnect?
SokoConnect is a digital platform that connects you with nearby Mama Mboga vendors, making it easy to order pre-prepared foods conveniently from your phone or computer.

2. How do I place an order?
You can place an order using the SokoConnect Progressive Web App. Simply browse available vendors and foods, add your selections to the cart, and follow the checkout instructions.

3. How are Mama Mbogas selected to fulfill my order?
When you order, your request is broadcast to all Mama Mbogas within a 500-meter radius. The nearest available vendor can accept and fulfill your order.

4. How are vendor ratings determined?
After each order, you’ll be asked to rate your experience on a 5-star scale. These ratings help ensure high quality and service standards.

5. What happens if I have an issue with my order?
You can submit feedback or raise a complaint directly within the app. Our support team will work to resolve issues promptly.

6. Are there any loyalty rewards for frequent buyers?
Yes! Regular customers automatically receive special discounts and loyalty offers from Mama Mbogas.

7. What payment methods are supported?
Payments can be made securely through M-Pesa directly within the app.

8. Is my personal data safe?
Absolutely. We employ robust security and data protection measures in compliance with Kenyan law to ensure your data is secure.

**For Mama Mboga Vendors**
1. How do I join SokoConnect?
Download the Mama Mboga App, complete your registration, and start updating your stock. Our team may assist you in onboarding.

2. What do I need to start selling?
You’ll need a smartphone, a valid phone number, and to provide basic business details to register as a vendor.

3. How do I receive orders?
Orders from customers within 500 meters are automatically broadcast to you. You can accept and fulfill orders using the app.

4. How does the rating system work?
Customers rate you after every transaction. If your rating falls below 3 stars, you’ll be prompted to take measures inorder to continue on our platform.

5. Can I offer discounts or loyalty rewards to customers?
Yes. You can create special offers or discounts for loyal customers, and the system will automatically apply these during purchases.

6. How do I update my stock or menu?
You can easily update your available products and prices directly in the mobile app.

**General**

We are expanding rapidly. Check the app or website to see if service is available in your location.
