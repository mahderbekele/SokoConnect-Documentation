SokoConnect Customer Guideline

Welcome to SokoConnect!

SokoConnect helps you find and order pre-prepared foods from trusted Mama Mboga vendors near you—all from your phone or computer. Here’s how to get started and make the most of your experience.

1. Getting Started
Access SokoConnect:
Download our app from playstore on your smartphone or tablet.

![alt text](<Images/Screenshot from 2025-07-25 09-56-08.png>)

Create Your Account:
Sign up by providing the required information and verifying your phone number.
![alt text](<Images/Screenshot from 2025-07-25 09-57-26.png>)

2. Browsing and Placing Orders
Find Vendors:
Browse through listed Mama Mboga vendors and discover what foods and offers are available in your area.

Order with Ease:
Add your preferred items to the cart and proceed to checkout. Orders are sent to Mama Mboga vendors within 500 meters for quick service.
![alt text](<Images/Screenshot from 2025-07-25 09-58-47.png>)

Order Confirmation:
Once your order is accepted, track its status in real time.

3. Payments
Secure Payments:
Pay safely through M-Pesa directly within the app. You’ll receive a confirmation for every transaction.
![alt text](<Images/Screenshot from 2025-07-25 10-01-14.png>)

Keep Your Records:
View your past orders and transactions anytime under your profile.


4. Ratings and Feedback
Rate Your Vendor:
After each order, leave a rating (1–5 stars) and feedback to help maintain high service quality.
![alt text](<Images/Screenshot from 2025-07-25 10-02-16.png>)

Reporting Issues:
Use the app’s feedback or complaint section to report any problems or share suggestions. Our team will follow up promptly.

5. Loyalty & Rewards
Earn Discounts:
Frequent purchases may unlock special offers or discounts that are automatically applied to your next order.

Stay Updated:
Check the “Offers” section in the app for the latest deals from your favorite Mama Mbogas.

6. Privacy and Security
Your Data is Safe:
We comply with Kenya’s data protection laws. Your personal data and transaction details are confidential and secure.

Manage Your Data:
Access, update, or request deletion of your personal data through your profile settings.

7. Tips for the Best Experience

Keep your profile and contact information up to date.

Regularly check your app for new offers and vendor ratings.

Provide constructive feedback to help vendors improve and earn rewards for yourself!

Enjoy fresh, convenient foods from your local Mama Mboga and contribute to building a fair, efficient, and modern food marketplace!
