__Introduction__

SokoConnect is a multi-vendor Platform designed to connect customers with multiple Mama Mboga vendors for convenient and efficient access to pre-prepared foods. This platform consists of two interfaces:

Mobile App for Mama Mbogas

Progressive Web App for customers

The Progressive Web App enables customers to browse, order, and rate the services provided by Mama-Mbogas, while Mama-Mbogas updates their stock and fulfills orders through a dedicated mobile App. This multi-vendor setup ensures a variety of pre-prepared foods and competitive pricing, benefiting both customers and vendors. 
Customers’ orders are broadcast to all Mama Mbogas within a 500-meter radius. This allows the closest available vendors to respond quickly and fulfill orders efficiently.
To enhance vendor quality and customer satisfaction, the system incorporates a rating mechanism where customers rate Mama Mbogas after each order. Vendors with ratings below a defined threshold (3 stars) receive automated notifications prompting them to attend a training session conducted by a partner (E-veggies). This ensures continuous improvement and maintains high service standards.
Additionally, the platform supports a loyalty and discount system where Mama Mbogas can offer special discounts. The system automatically applies these offers to loyal customers who frequently purchase through the app, promoting customer retention and rewarding consistent buyers.

__Problem Statement__

Mama Mbogas, informal grocery vendors in Kenya, need digital solutions because they are losing touch with youth customers who prefer online shopping.

__Users of our platform__

Mama Mboga vendors- informal vegetable sellers who play a vital role in Kenya’s urban food supply, particularly for low-income communities. 

Young customers- who prefer online shopping to physical shopping.
