__Introduction__

This description is about SokoConnect, a multi-vendor digital platform designed to connect informal vegetable vendors in Kenya (called Mama Mbogas) with young customers who prefer shopping online.

__Purpose__

To provide a convenient way for customers to browse, order, and rate pre-prepared foods supplied by multiple Mama Mboga vendors.

__Users__

Mama Mboga vendors: Informal vegetable sellers who manage stocks and fulfill orders via a mobile app.

Young customers: Use a Progressive Web App to browse vendors, place orders, and rate their experience.

__How it works__

Customers’ orders are broadcast to all vendors within a 500-meter radius, enabling nearby vendors to quickly respond and fulfill orders.

Check the [document](Documentation/Introduction.md) for more information.
